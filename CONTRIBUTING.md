# Contributing

Questions, ideas and findings from other tables (models, firmware versions, timings) are welcome in
[Discussions](../../discussions). Pull requests for docs, test data and the Swift code are welcome too. CI
runs `swift run DriftedKitChecks` and `scripts/check-publishable.sh`.

By contributing, you agree to the contribution terms in [LEGAL.md](LEGAL.md#contributions). In short:
your contribution is licensed under the MIT License, it's yours to give, and it contains no HoMedics
software, firmware, code derived from them or stock patterns, nothing obtained in breach of an agreement, and no
photos of people without their permission.
